From your first Botox appointment to a full collagen-restoration plan, you will always see the same nurse who knows your face, your goals, and your history.

Botox

Smooth frown lines, crow's feet, and forehead creases. Results appear within 24 to 48 hours and last three to four months. The most-requested treatment in the practice.

$12 per unit

Dysport

A finely-targeted neurotoxin that reduces muscle activity between the brows. A lighter feel than Botox for some patients, with the same precision placement.

$12 per unit

Juvederm Fillers

Hyaluronic acid filler that restores volume, softens deep creases, and defines contours. Juvederm can hold up to 1,000 times its weight in water for smooth, lasting results.

Starting at $475

Sculptra

A bio-stimulator that rebuilds your own collagen gradually over two to three sessions. Results develop over months and can last up to two years. Janelle's preferred long-game treatment.

From $750 per vial

Kybella

The only FDA-approved injectable that permanently destroys submental fat cells. A non-surgical solution for a defined jawline and reduced fullness under the chin.

$450 per vial

PRF, CoolSculpting & More

Undereye PRF uses your own platelet-rich fibrin for natural renewal. CoolSculpting contours the body without surgery. Spider vein injections and GLP-1 weight-loss coaching round out the menu.

From $300 (consult required)

About Janelle

21 years as a nurse.
18 of them in aesthetics.

CPSN CANS Allergan Medical Aesthetics Trainer Nurse of the Year 2009 Registered Nurse, Wisconsin

When you come to Genesis by Janelle, you meet Janelle. Not a rotating schedule of providers. Not a med-school student doing a rotation. The same Certified Plastic Surgery Nurse and Certified Aesthetic Nurse Specialist who has been doing this work since before most medspas in the area existed.

Those two certifications, CPSN and CANS, are not the same thing. They are administered by separate bodies, require separate practice hours, and cover different scopes. Holding both is uncommon. Working at this level in a single independent suite, where every patient gets a full consultation with the practitioner whose name is on the door, is rarer still.

Janelle trained with Allergan, the maker of Botox and Juvederm, and has served as a trainer herself. In 2009 she was named Nurse of the Year. She is supported by a small, hand-selected team at 3852 Creamery Road in De Pere, including nurse practitioners and a licensed aesthetician with 17 years of experience.

If you have tried injections elsewhere and felt rushed, or if this is your first time considering aesthetics and you want to take it slowly, this is the right place.
